## Drafted by Haven, with no direct source yet

Please check these lines closely.

- Do not confront the person you are worried about.
- Do not promise to keep it secret.
- Write down what you saw, when you saw it, and what the child said.
- Bring the clothes the child was wearing.
- Do not forward, save or share a sexual picture of a child, even as proof. Reporting practice and Nigerian law should be checked here.
- Do not shout, shame the child, or tell them it is a sin.
- All of the physical harm, grooming and "a child has changed" steps, which apply the communication guidance to those situations.
- The questions and the urgency each answer leads to.
- The word lists in `src/lib/triage.ts` that trigger an emergency.
